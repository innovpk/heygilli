HeyGilli — Privacy Policy

Effective: 7 September 2026  ·  Last updated: 7 September 2026
Contact: hello@heygilli.com
HeyGilli is an app for parents that screens YouTube channels for a child and keeps a buddy character alongside them while they watch. This page explains, in full, what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how to have it deleted. It is written for a parent to actually read — where something is not built yet, it says so, rather than describing a promise instead of the product.

1. Who this covers

HeyGilli is operated as an independent project, not a company. Throughout this policy, "we," "us," and "HeyGilli" refer to that project, reachable at hello@heygilli.com.

Only a parent or guardian sets HeyGilli up — though there is no account to create and nothing to sign in to. A child never signs in to anything, is never asked to enter information, and is never shown a way to leave their own screen without the parent's PIN. Everything in this policy about "you" refers to the parent.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account identity

There is no account. Starting a household generates a random identifier on your device — something like trial-3c994baf52eab4ec… — which is stored there and sent to our server so the same device finds the same household next time. No name, no email, no password, and no Google account is collected.

Sign in with Google is not currently offered. The app contains the code for it, but it is switched off: it only ever read the parent's own subscriptions, and a Takeout export reaches more — including the children's own profiles — without asking for access to anyone's Google account. Nothing on this page describes data we take from Google, because at present we take none.

2.2 Your child's profile

You create this; your child does not enter any of it themselves.

  • Nickname — whatever you choose to call them in the app. We do not ask for a legal name.
  • Age (3–12), which we convert into a fixed age band (4–6, 7–8, 9–11) used to decide what Gilli says and whether text is shown at all.
  • Language preference(s) for the videos and for Gilli's speech.
  • An avatar choice (a small set of built-in options — no photo of your child is ever collected).
  • Settings you choose: which channels are approved, daily time limits, break timing, and whether the search box is shown to them.

2.3 YouTube subscriptions and viewing history

Neither YouTube nor YouTube Kids exposes an API that reads a child's profile, so the only route to what your children actually follow is the standard export Google itself produces at takeout.google.com. You download it and choose it here.

Before anything leaves your device, the app strips that export down to just the subscription lists — channel ids and names. Watch history, search history, comments and everything else in it are discarded on your device and never uploaded, unless you separately opt in below. You can also skip the export entirely and paste in channels one at a time.

Watch history is opt-in, every single time, and is never stored as a list. If you tick the history option during an import, we read the exported "watch-history.json" only long enough to compute a small aggregate — total videos, the date range, a per-hour-of-day pattern, and the channels most and least watched — and then discard the underlying list of individual videos entirely. We do not retain a video-by-video watch history for any child, on our servers or otherwise.

2.4 What happens while your child is watching

  • Session records — which video was shown, for how long, on what date, and on which type of device (phone, tablet, web). This is how time limits and the parent digest are built.
  • Spoken answers, as text — never as audio. When Gilli asks a question and your child answers out loud, speech recognition happens on the device itself. Only the recognized text (for example, "giraffe") is sent to our server to check understanding; the audio recording itself is not sent anywhere and is not retained by the app.
  • Words said and heard — short word lists (for example, words a pre-reader successfully echoed back) used to build the daily summary you see as a parent.
  • Gilli's own spoken lines are synthesized as audio by Amazon Polly (see §4) each time they're needed — this is generated speech, not a recording of anyone.

2.5 What never leaves your device

  • Your parent PIN, used only to leave a child's device — it is never transmitted to our servers in any form.
  • Which physical device belongs to which child (so a tablet handed to a child opens straight into their videos).
  • Whether you've already seen the intro screens.

3. How we use this information

  • To screen new uploads from approved channels before they can ever appear to your child (age-appropriateness and language screening).
  • To run Gilli's spoken questions and check understanding during and after a video.
  • To enforce the daily time limits and breaks you set.
  • To build the digest and progress views shown only to you, the parent.
  • To keep the app working — remembering your household on this device and keeping your children's settings.

We do not use your or your child's information to build advertising profiles, and we do not run any advertising or third-party analytics or tracking SDKs of any kind in the app.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing purposes. Information passes through the following service providers, strictly to run the app:

ProviderWhat it handles
Amazon Web Services — Bedrock The AI model that screens videos and drafts Gilli's questions. It sees video metadata and transcripts, not your account identity.
Amazon Web Services — Polly Converts Gilli's scripted lines to speech audio.
Amazon Web Services — DynamoDB Stores household, child, and session records described above.
Render & Vercel Host the server and the app itself. Standard web server logs (IP address, timestamp, requested address) are generated at this layer, as with any web service.

We may disclose information if required to by law, or to protect the safety of a child, ourselves, or others — this is a standard reservation, not something we expect to need.

We receive nothing from Google's APIs. With Google sign-in switched off, no Google account data reaches HeyGilli at all. Your channels come from an export you download and upload yourself, or from channels you type in. If that ever changes, this policy will say so before it does, and use of any Google data would follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

5. Children's privacy

HeyGilli is built around the requirement that a child never creates an household, never signs in, and never provides information directly — every child profile is created and configured by a parent, and every consent to upload an export is given by that parent. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from a child.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information other than through a parent setting up their own household, contact us at hello@heygilli.com and we will investigate and delete it.

6. How long we keep information, and how to delete it

We keep household and child records for as long as the household exists, so the app can keep working the way you left it. Some things you can remove yourself right now inside the app:

  • Removing a channel from a child (Channels → remove) deletes that approval immediately.
  • Removing an imported watch-history aggregate for a child, from that child's history screen.

Deleting everything is not yet self-serve. HeyGilli does not currently have an in-app delete button. To request deletion of a household, a child's profile, or anything else we hold, email hello@heygilli.com and describe the household so we can locate it — there is no account name to quote, so tell us the nicknames and roughly when you set it up. We aim to complete this within 30 days.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). This project is at an early, actively developed stage — as with any software at this stage, our security practices continue to evolve, and we do not claim a specific certification or guarantee against every possible failure. We ask that you pick a PIN you don't reuse for anything sensitive elsewhere.

8. Your choices

  • Decide per import whether to include watch history at all — it is off by default, every time.
  • Decide per child whether they see a search box.
  • Remove any channel or the history aggregate at any time.
  • Request full deletion by email at any time (§6).

9. International use

Our infrastructure runs on servers located in the United States (AWS us-east-1). If you use HeyGilli from outside the United States, your information is transferred to and processed there.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that meaningfully affects what we collect or how we use it, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use HeyGilli after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions, deletion requests, or concerns about a child's information: hello@heygilli.com.
