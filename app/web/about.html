What it does

Four things — and your child never signs in to any of them.

📺

You choose the channels

Nothing reaches your child from a channel you haven't approved. No open-ended search unless you turn it on.

🔍

Every upload is screened

New videos are checked automatically against the rules and limits you set, before they can appear.

💬

Gilli watches along

A spoken buddy asks a question or two during and after a video, pitched to your child's age and language.

📊

You get the digest

What they watched, what they understood, what they found hard — shown only to you.

Why it asks for your YouTube subscriptions

Neither YouTube nor YouTube Kids exposes a way to read a child's profile. So HeyGilli reads your account's subscriptions — read-only, never posting or changing anything — to build the list of channels you already trust, which you approve one by one.

You can skip that entirely: import a Google Takeout export, or add channels yourself.

What your child never does

  • Sign in, or enter their name
  • Reach a channel you haven't allowed
  • Get recommended anything you didn't approve
  • Have their voice uploaded — speech is recognised on the device

Watch history is off by default. The privacy policy says exactly what is kept.
