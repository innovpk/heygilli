Why it asks for your YouTube subscriptions

Nothing exposes a child's YouTube Kids profile, so HeyGilli reads your account's subscriptions — read-only, never posting or changing anything — to build the list of channels you already trust. Or skip it: import a Takeout export, or add channels yourself.

What your child never does

  • Sign in, or enter their name
  • Reach a channel you haven't allowed
  • Have their voice uploaded — speech is recognised on the device
