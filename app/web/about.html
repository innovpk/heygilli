How HeyGilli works Gilli is watching along
Tonight · from Gilli

Understood: heat makes things move.

Still shaky: what "erupted" means.

Ask at dinner: "What made the lava come out?"

1

Answer a few questions

Unboxing? Sponsors? Mild scares? Once, at setup. Change them any time.

2

Every upload gets read

From channels you approved only. What fits, plays. What's borderline comes to you, with the reason.

3

Two lines at bedtime

What they understood, what's shaky, one thing to ask at dinner. Not a dashboard.

The buddy

Gilli asks what they made of it

At a natural pause, one question. A four-year-old taps a picture; a nine-year-old answers in a sentence. Gilli says the answer back either way, so a wrong guess still gets heard properly.

Nobody is told they are wrong.

What we don't keep

No child account

A nickname you choose and an age. No legal name, no photo, no sign-in.

Never the audio

Only the recognised words reach us. On Android, recognition happens on the device itself; in a browser it is the browser's own recogniser, and Chrome does that by sending audio to Google.

No Google needed

Sign in if you like — we get your name and email, not your subscriptions and not your history.

Search off by default

History is opt-in per import, and only an aggregate is kept — the video-by-video list is discarded as it is read.

The privacy policy says exactly what is kept, in the same plain words. Videos play in YouTube's own player, ads and all — creators are paid exactly as they would be.

Why not YouTube Kids?

It sorts by broad age band and builds a feed. Fine — until the thing you care about isn't an age band.
