3. What HeyGilli is — and is not

HeyGilli screens YouTube channels you approve, using an automated system, and keeps a buddy character alongside your child while they watch, asking questions about what's on screen.

The screening is automated and imperfect. Videos are evaluated by an AI system against the rules you set, not reviewed by a person, and it can make mistakes in either direction — approving something you wouldn't have wanted, or holding back something harmless. HeyGilli is a tool that helps you supervise, not a replacement for actually knowing what your child watches. Use it alongside your own judgment, not instead of it.

HeyGilli only ever shows videos from channels you have explicitly approved. It does not browse YouTube on your child's behalf beyond those channels, and your child is never shown a way to search or reach channels you haven't approved (unless you specifically turn that on).
