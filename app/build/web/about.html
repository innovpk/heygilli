HeyGilli

A buddy who watches YouTube with your kid.

HeyGilli is an app for parents. You pick the YouTube channels your child is allowed to watch; every new upload from those channels is screened automatically before it can ever appear to your child. While they watch, a buddy character named Gilli sits alongside them — asking a question or two about what just happened, in your child's own language — and afterwards tells you what they understood and what they found hard.

What it does

You choose the channels

Nothing reaches your child from a channel you haven't approved. No open-ended search unless you turn it on.

Every upload is screened

New videos from approved channels are checked automatically, using rules and limits you set, before they can appear.

Gilli watches along

A spoken buddy asks a question or two during and after a video, suited to your child's age and language.

You get the digest

What they watched, what they understood, and what they found hard — shown only to you, never to your child.

Why it needs your YouTube subscriptions

Neither YouTube nor YouTube Kids has a way to read a child's profile directly. HeyGilli asks to read your Google account's YouTube subscriptions (read-only — it never posts, uploads, or changes anything) so it can build the list of channels you already trust, which you then approve for your child one by one. You can also bring channels across from a Google Takeout export instead, and you can always add channels yourself without connecting Google at all.

Get started

Open the app and sign in as a parent — your child never signs in to anything.
